# Households

**Route** `/saves/:saveFileId/households` ·
**Tier** 1 ·
**Requires** no pack (University rows inside a sim need Discover University) ·
**Polish** shippable, with one blemish: the two rail buttons don't match the
app's button grammar ·
**Verified** 2026-08-12 by reading every file for this surface end to end —
`HouseholdWorkspace.tsx`, `SimPanel.tsx`, `CreateHouseholdModal.tsx`,
`HouseholdBuiltPhotos.tsx`, `ImportPortraitsModal.tsx`, `LotPickerModal.tsx`,
the store (`useSaveFile.ts`), the sync rules (`diff.ts`, `plannedMoveSync.ts`,
`snapshot.ts`, `coherence.ts`) and the server's household routes — plus driving
the page and its edit modes.

> **This is the sim editor as well as the household editor.** Every sim lives in
> a household, so a household is how you reach one. Column 3 is a full per-sim
> editor with seven sections. Describing only the household half misses most of
> the screen.

## Why it exists

A Sims save holds far more households than a player cares about — the save fills
with townies and service sims whether you want them or not. This screen
separates the ones that are yours from the ones that merely exist, and it's where
you decide what happens next: who lives where, who moves, what a sim is working
towards.

## What you see

Three columns. Below large screens the first two collapse into a drawer behind a
**Households** button, so the sim editor keeps its width.

**Column 1 — the list.** **New household** and **Sync photos**, a search box,
and a world filter. Then an accordion with one zone open at a time: **My
Households** (with a count) or **Rest of Town** (with its own). Searching
overrides the accordion and shows matches from both, each under its own label.
Rows show a portrait — imported from the game, or a green-to-purple gradient
placeholder — the name, and the member count. Arrow keys walk the list.

Two filters appear only when they'd do something: **Save** / **Planner** chips on
My Households when you have both kinds, and provenance pills on Rest of Town when
more than one kind is present.

**Provenance badges** are a six-value vocabulary, each with its own explanation
on hover:

| Badge | Means |
|---|---|
| **Yours** | A household you created, downloaded from the gallery, or a premade you've made yours by playing, editing or moving a sim in |
| **Premade** | From the game, and you haven't touched it |
| **Townie** | Generated by the game |
| **Service Sim** | Game-run, like the Maid or the Grim Reaper |
| **Mod** | Added by a mod |
| **Plan-only** | In your plan, not your save |

On My Households rows the only badge is a purple **Planner** for households you
made here.

**Column 2 — the household.** Cover photo, name, the lot and its world, and
funds. Then **Members**, and **Moving out** / **Moving in** when planned moves
exist. Then the **description** from the save, hidden if there isn't one.
**Showcase Photos**, collapsed with a count. **Notes**, private. An **Edit**
button that becomes **Done**, and a purple **↺ N** when fields differ from the
save.

**Column 3 — the sim.** Avatar tinted by gender and faded if they're a ghost, the
name, a **From save** or **From planner** badge, and a role badge for service
sims. Then: **Household**, **Identity** (First · Last · Gender · Lifestage ·
Occult · Died of), **Aspiration & Traits**, **Skills**, **Career & Education**
(Career · University · Degrees), **Memberships** (Clubs · Dynasty · Business),
and **Notes**. Its own **Edit**/**Done** toggle and **↺ N**.

Before you pick a household, column 3 shows **Town at a glance** — households
split into yours and Rest of Town, total sims, and how many are on a lot. Pick a
household but no sim and it says *Select a sim to edit*.

## What you can do

**With a household** — lot, funds, name and member changes are behind **Edit**

- Create one, and add sims to it
- Rename it
- Assign it to a lot, change the lot, or unassign it
- Set a **funds target** to reach. Type the save's own figure and the target
  clears itself back to just mirroring
- Write private notes
- Upload showcase photos, each with a caption and a gallery creator
- Revert the lot or the funds target to the save's value, singly or together
- Delete it — only if you made it here

**With a sim** — everything except Notes is behind the panel's **Edit**

- Change **First**, **Last** and **Gender**
- Set **Occult**, which includes **Ghost**, and **Died of**. Naming a cause of
  death makes them a ghost; bringing them back to life clears the cause. Every
  occult comes from a pack, so you're offered the ones you own — Ghost is base
  game and always offered. A sim who already is one keeps it listed whatever
  you own
- Change **Lifestage** — *only on sims you created here*
- Choose an **Aspiration** and edit **Traits**, capped by lifestage: one for
  infants, toddlers and children, two for teens, three for adults
- Add **planned skills** — skills you intend them to learn
- Author a **Career**, or mark them as planned unemployed. The list is the jobs
  their age can hold — teens are offered the teen jobs, adults full-time,
  part-time and freelance work under those three headings. Children and under
  have no Career row at all
- Set a **University** enrolment and add **earned degrees** (Discover University)
- Plan a move to another household — existing or a new one named on the spot
- Write private notes
- Revert any edited field to the save's value, or all of them at once

**With portraits** — **Sync photos** reads your game's
`localthumbcache.package` and matches household photos to this save. It tells you
how many of the file's photos matched.

## What you can't

- **Use it on a phone.** Narrow touch devices get a "works best on desktop"
  screen. A resized desktop browser is fine.
- **Delete anything from your save.** Only plan-only households and sims can be
  deleted — a save-backed one would come back on the next sync.
- **Re-age a sim from your save.** Lifestage is read-only for them, because
  re-aging would cascade through their traits, skills and career. Sims you
  created here can be any age.
- **Plan a skill they already have.** Skills from the save are locked; planned
  skills are only ones they don't have yet.
- **Set a skill or career *level*.** You choose which skills and which career;
  levels are the game's to report.
- **Un-earn a degree.** Degrees from the save are locked. Ones you added here
  can be removed.
- **Move a sim you invented.** Only sims that exist in the save can carry a
  planned move.
- **Empty a household**, or put more than **8 sims** in one — both are the game's
  rules.
- **Assign a household to a full lot.** The lot picker hides them, and only
  Residential Rentals hold more than one household. **Reverting the lot to the
  save's value is refused the same way** — if you've since moved another
  household into the save's lot, the revert says *"<lot> is full"* and leaves
  both households where they are, rather than putting two in one house. Free the
  lot and the revert works.
- **Edit Clubs, Dynasty or Business here.** Memberships are shown, not edited.
- **Get a portrait for a household you invented.** Photos match on the game's own
  household id, which a plan-only household doesn't have.
- **Get portraits for individual sims.** The game caches household photos only.

## How it relates to sync

**Mirrored from your save**, refreshed every sync: a household's name, members,
description and lot; a sim's name, gender, lifestage, species, occult, ghost
state, cause of death, traits, aspiration, university enrolment, **career**, and
which household they're in.

**Yours, and never overwritten**: notes, showcase photos, funds targets, planned
moves, planned skills, authored careers, and every household or sim you created
here. Plan-only records are invisible to the sync entirely.

**Two things refresh without ever appearing in a change count**, because they're
deliberately kept out of the comparison: **household money** and **sim skills**.
This is why the sync screen promises that skills and funds refresh even when it
reports nothing new.

**If you edited a field and the game didn't touch it, your edit survives** — the
sync skips it rather than undoing you. This is settled **per field, not per
household**: a household you renamed whose members changed in-game keeps your
name and takes the new members, and the same holds for every field on a sim. A
household appearing in the sync screen's count is not a household that got
overwritten.

**Planned moves resolve themselves.** A move clears when that sim's household
changed in the game since the last sync — whether or not they went where you
planned, because moving is deliberate and reality outranks the plan. It does not
clear if the sim has vanished from the save, and never on a first sync, since
there's nothing to compare against.

**You are never asked to choose.** No review queue, no conflict list, no per-item
approval.

**Deleting a household doesn't necessarily delete its sims.** Members with family
relationships are kept as tree-only records so your family tree survives; the
rest go. Planned moves pointing at that household are cleared.

**Re-running Sync photos** only touches households it matched, so a thinner cache
can't wipe portraits it doesn't contain — but a matched household's existing
portrait is replaced.
